Privacy Policy

Job Radar (Google Cloud app name: "jobs") · Last updated 27 September 2026

Job Radar is a personal tool built and used only by Alwaleed Alotaibi to follow his own job applications. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads messages in the owner's own Gmail account using the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It looks only for LinkedIn job alerts, application confirmations, replies from employers, and applications the owner sent. It cannot send, delete, or change any email.

What it stores

It saves short extracts (date, sender or recipient, subject, company, job title) in a private GitHub repository owned by the owner, so his job tracker can show them. Full email bodies are not stored.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
